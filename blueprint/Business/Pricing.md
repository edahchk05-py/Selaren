# Pricing

## Philosophy

Selaren should be priced against the **financial value of recovered patients**, not against cheap clinic-management software.

If one additional high-value patient can justify the cost, pricing should sit in that economic range — not in the range of generic dental admin tools.

## Initial strategic targets

These are **not** fixed prices.

| Component | Target range |
| --- | --- |
| One-time setup | 8,000–15,000 MAD |
| Monthly SaaS | 3,500–8,000 MAD |

Monthly price may depend on clinic size, volume, and functionality.

## How to use the range

- Use the range in strategy and sales thinking.
- Do not treat either bound as a public SKU.
- Do not invent tiers, add-ons, or usage fees that are not defined here.

Actual numbers for each clinic or pilot require **founder approval**.

## Value anchor

Price should remain understandable as:

> Less than the value of the demand you are currently losing.

Do not compete by being the cheapest software in the clinic.

## What not to do

- Do not default to “starter / pro / enterprise” packaging without evidence.
- Do not price as a chatbot or AI-message product.
- Do not underprice to match PMS tools.
- Do not publish a price page until the founder decides to.

## Commercial (outside the product)

Exact clinic prices and whether a 14-day pilot is paid remain founder-to-clinic agreements. They are not encoded in the app.

Do not productize billing in the MVP.
