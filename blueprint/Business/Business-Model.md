# Business Model

## Type

B2B SaaS, sold initially through in-person sales to premium private dental clinics in Casablanca and Marrakech.

## Initial model

Two components:

1. **One-time setup fee**
2. **Monthly SaaS subscription**

These are strategic targets, not a published price list. See `blueprint/Business/Pricing.md`.

## What the customer is paying for

Not “software seats” in the abstract.

The clinic is paying for conversion of inbound demand into appointments and revenue — recovered patients after acquisition spend.

## Setup fee (role)

The setup fee exists because onboarding is not self-serve at this stage.

It should cover clinic-specific work required to make conversion work: workspace, WhatsApp connection, knowledge/context, availability, and working with the front desk.

Exact setup scope is a founder decision.

## Monthly SaaS (role)

Recurring fee for continued use of the conversion system: inbox, AI-assisted responses, qualification, booking, follow-up, and basic analytics.

Price may vary by clinic size, volume, and functionality. Ranges are targets, not rules.

## What this model is not

- not mass-market cheap clinic-management pricing
- not per-message AI usage as the primary commercial story
- not a free consumer app
- not multi-country packaging

## Billing infrastructure

Do not build billing infrastructure unless it is required to run the MVP.

Invoices and pilot terms are **manual, founder-owned**. The MVP does not include billing infrastructure.

## Constraint

Sell before scaling. Prove financial value in pilots before expanding the model (new packages, new verticals, new countries).
