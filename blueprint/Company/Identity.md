# Identity

This file is part of the Selaren company blueprint — the source of truth for what Selaren is. Do not contradict it in product, sales, or implementation work.

## Company

**Selaren**

## Tagline

Turn every inquiry into an appointment.

## Category

B2B SaaS.

Selaren is a **patient-demand conversion system**.

The deeper category, over time, is **high-intent demand conversion**.

## What Selaren is

Selaren helps premium clinics convert high-intent patient inquiries into booked appointments — and, where applicable, deposits and show-ups.

Selaren exists to improve this loop:

**Inquiry → Response → Qualification → Appointment → Deposit → Show-up**

## What Selaren is not

Selaren is not:

- a generic chatbot
- an AI receptionist
- a clinic management system (PMS)
- a medical records system
- an accounting system
- a generic CRM
- an ERP
- a generic “AI employee”

AI is an implementation technology, not the category.

## Initial market

- **Country:** Morocco
- **Cities:** Casablanca, Marrakech
- **Vertical:** Premium private dental clinics

Do not expand country, city, or vertical until the founder decides to.

## Initial treatments in scope

High-value dental treatments, including:

- dental implants
- veneers
- aligners
- orthodontics
- aesthetic dentistry
- other high-value treatments offered by the clinic

## Outcome

More qualified inquiries → more booked appointments → more revenue.
