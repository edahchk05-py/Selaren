# Vision

## Near-term vision

Become the system premium private dental clinics in Casablanca and Marrakech use to convert inbound patient demand into appointments.

The initial wedge is dental. The company is not “dental software.”

## Expansion path

Expansion is sequential and founder-approved:

1. Premium private dental clinics (current)
2. Aesthetic / dermatology clinics
3. Other high-ticket private services
4. International markets

Do not build for later stages now.

## Long-term category

Selaren should become infrastructure that helps high-value businesses turn inbound demand into revenue.

The durable category is:

**High-intent demand conversion.**

## What the company should become

A focused conversion system that:

- captures demand the business already paid to generate
- responds quickly
- qualifies
- books
- follows up
- measures financial outcomes

## What the company must not become

- a generic AI wrapper
- a clinic PMS
- a medical records platform
- an accounting or ERP system
- a mass-market chatbot for every dentist

## Constraint

Stay obsessed with the inquiry-to-appointment conversion loop. Breadth of verticals comes after proof in one.
