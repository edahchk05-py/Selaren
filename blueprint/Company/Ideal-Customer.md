# Ideal Customer

## Initial customer

Premium private dental clinics.

Do not target every dentist.

## Geographic focus

- Morocco
- Casablanca
- Marrakech

## Ideal clinic characteristics

The clinic should be:

- private
- premium-positioned
- actively acquiring patients
- receiving meaningful WhatsApp / Instagram / website inquiries
- staffed with a receptionist, secretary, or front desk
- generating enough revenue to pay a recurring SaaS subscription
- losing opportunities because of slow responses, missed inquiries, weak follow-up, or poor booking conversion

## Economic test

The ideal customer is a clinic where **one additional high-value patient can justify the cost of Selaren**.

If recovered demand cannot pay for the product, the clinic is not a fit.

## Priority treatments

- dental implants
- veneers
- aligners
- orthodontics
- aesthetic dentistry
- other high-value treatments

## Who we sell to inside the clinic

Primary: clinic owner / decision-maker.

Day-to-day users: receptionist, secretary, or front desk.

Observe both. The owner feels lost revenue. The front desk lives the inquiry workflow.

## Who we do not target (now)

- public or low-cost high-volume practices as the primary ICP
- clinics with no meaningful inbound inquiry volume
- clinics that are not actively acquiring patients
- every general dentist by default
- aesthetic/dermatology clinics (future vertical, not now)
- businesses outside Morocco (not now)

## Future customers

Later verticals may include aesthetic/dermatology clinics and other high-ticket businesses.

Do not design, sell, or build for them until the founder decides to expand.
