# Positioning

## Primary positioning

Selaren helps premium clinics turn high-intent patient inquiries into booked appointments.

## Core emotional message

Don't lose the patient you already paid to attract.

## Primary tagline

Turn every inquiry into an appointment.

## Category language

Use:

- patient-demand conversion system
- inquiry-to-appointment conversion

Do **not** position Selaren as:

- an AI receptionist
- a chatbot company
- clinic management software
- a CRM
- an “AI employee”

AI is how some responses may be assisted. It is not what Selaren is.

## Contrast

| Clinics already have | Selaren is for |
| --- | --- |
| Ads, Instagram, Google, website, WhatsApp | What happens after the patient inquires |
| Reception / front desk | Making that demand convert |
| Clinic tools for records, billing, operations | Booking the patient, not running the clinic |

## Sales frame

The clinic is not buying software features.

The clinic is buying recovered patients: inquiries that would otherwise go unanswered, unqualified, or unbooked.

Price against that value. See `blueprint/Business/Pricing.md`.

## Language to avoid

- “AI receptionist”
- “replaces your secretary”
- “clinic operating system”
- “all-in-one dental software”
- “autonomous agent for your clinic”

## Proof, not claims

Positioning must be backed by pilot measurement: response time, inquiries, qualification, appointments, deposits, follow-ups, and conversion.

Do not lead with AI. Lead with conversion outcomes.
