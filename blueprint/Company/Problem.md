# Problem

## The situation

Premium private clinics spend money to attract potential patients through:

- Instagram
- Google
- WhatsApp
- websites
- phone calls

Patients then inquire: they ask questions, request prices, ask about treatments, or want to book.

## Where value is lost

The problem happens **after** the inquiry.

The clinic may:

- respond too slowly
- miss the message
- fail to follow up
- fail to qualify the patient
- fail to convert the conversation into an appointment

The acquisition cost has already been paid. The lead is wasted at conversion.

## The opportunity

Improve the conversion process:

**Patient inquiry → Response → Qualification → Appointment → Deposit → Show-up**

## Why this is painful for the ideal clinic

For a premium clinic, one additional high-value patient can justify a meaningful software cost.

Missed implants, veneers, aligners, orthodontics, or aesthetic cases are not small leaks. They are lost revenue on demand the clinic already bought.

## What this is not

This is not a “clinics need more AI” problem.

This is not a clinic-operations problem (records, billing, inventory, payroll).

This is a **demand-conversion** problem: inbound interest that never becomes a deposited, attended appointment.

## Evidence standard

Do not assume the size, causes, or frequency of leakage.

Confirm them through in-clinic observation and customer discovery before expanding the product. See `blueprint/GTM/Customer-Discovery.md`.
