# Go-to-Market

## Initial strategy

**In-person sales.**

The founder visits clinics, speaks with owners and receptionists, observes the current workflow, identifies missed inquiries, and offers a measured pilot.

## Where

- Morocco
- Casablanca
- Marrakech

Do not run a multi-city or national campaign until the founder decides to.

## Who

Premium private dental clinics that match `blueprint/Company/Ideal-Customer.md`.

Do not target every dentist.

## What not to rely on

Do not rely primarily on mass Instagram DMs.

Inbound social may exist later. It is not the initial acquisition engine.

## Initial pilot

- **Duration:** approximately 14 days
- **Objective:** prove measurable financial value

### Measure

- response time
- inquiries
- qualified inquiries
- appointments
- deposits
- follow-ups
- conversion

Do not declare the pilot a success because the AI “handled conversations.” Success is conversion and recovered demand.

## Sales motion

1. Get in the clinic.
2. Understand how inquiries arrive and how they are handled today.
3. Find leakage (slow replies, missed threads, no follow-up, no booking).
4. Offer a bounded pilot with measurement.
5. Review results with the owner in financial terms.

## Positioning in the room

Lead with: don’t lose the patient you already paid to attract.

Do not lead with: AI receptionist.

## Constraint

Sell before scaling.

Customer relationships, discovery, and sales belong to the founder. Cursor does not invent campaigns, scripts as policy, or new channels.
